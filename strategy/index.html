Chord once the flags are proven.

When the adjacent flags match a number, tap that number to open its remaining neighbours. Chording trusts your flags; it does not check whether they are correct.

Follow the coastline.

Strait boards use the same eight-neighbour rule, including diagonals. Land removes neighbours. Count the actual water tiles before applying an edge pattern.

A zero counter is not proof.

The counter subtracts placed flags, including wrong ones. Only treat the remaining tiles as safe when every flag is justified and every mine is accounted for.
