Beginner tips

Four tips, then the full pattern guides
  1. Work outward from the opening. The numbers on the edge of a cleared patch hold all the information. Start where a number has only one or two hidden tiles beside it.
  2. Only flag what you can prove. A wrong flag makes every number around it lie to you. If you're not sure, leave the tile and look elsewhere.
  3. Use the narrow water. Tiles next to land have fewer neighbours, so their numbers say more. Channels and coves are often where a board breaks open.
  4. Stuck? Look somewhere else. Every board here is no-guess: there is always a tile you can work out without guessing, somewhere on the board.

Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The game on this page is free to play, with no download and no account. The page shows ads around the game, never inside it.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in your browser. The first time you press Play, the game downloads about 35 MB; your browser keeps it, so later visits load faster.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. Tap to reveal, hold to flag, and pinch or use the zoom slider on the larger boards. Press Full screen for more room.

Will I ever have to guess?

No. Your first tap is always safe, and every board is generated so it can be solved by logic alone. If you're stuck, there is a safe move you can work out somewhere on the board.

What makes it different from classic minesweeper?

The boards are shaped by the real coastline of the Strait of Hormuz. The rules are the same, but land cuts the board into channels and coves, so some tiles have fewer neighbours and the logic flows differently.

Does my progress save?

Yes, in this browser. Your best time and wins for each board and difficulty are saved on your device, never on a server, and show in the game's board menu. They don't carry over to other browsers or to the app, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.
